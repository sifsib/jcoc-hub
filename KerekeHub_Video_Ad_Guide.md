## STEP 3 — CREATE THE TWO VIDEOS IN CANVA

### How to start each one:
1. Go to canva.com → click **Create a design**
2. Search for **"Presentation Video"** for the desktop version
3. Search for **"TikTok Video"** for the phone version
4. Choose a clean, modern dark or minimal template

---

## VIDEO 1 — DESKTOP/LAPTOP AD (16:9 ratio | 1920x1080)

**Length target: 45–60 seconds**

Use this as your Canva Magic Design prompt or as a brief when customising a template:

---

### CANVA AI PROMPT (Desktop Version):
```
Create a 45-second product demo video for a church administration SaaS app called Kereke Hub. 
Style: clean, professional, modern tech. 
Colour scheme: dark green #1A5C38, mid green #2E7D4F, white text, light green accents. 
Format: 16:9 widescreen. 
Structure: bold opening title screen → 7 quick feature highlight slides (each 4–5 seconds) → closing call-to-action screen. 
Font: modern sans-serif (Poppins or Inter). 
Include space on each slide for a screenshot/mockup on the right and text on the left. 
Animated transitions between slides. Professional, not playful.
```

## VIDEO 2 — TIKTOK/PHONE AD (9:16 ratio | 1080x1920)

**Length target: 20–30 seconds** (TikTok/Reels audiences scroll fast)

---

### CANVA AI PROMPT (TikTok Version):
```
Create a 25-second vertical social media video (9:16 ratio) for a church administration app called Kereke Hub. 
Style: modern, bold, fast-paced. 
Colour scheme: dark green #1A5C38, white text, green accents. 
Structure: punchy hook text screen (3 sec) → 6 quick feature screens stacked vertically (each 3 sec) → closing screen with CTA. 
Each feature screen: full-bleed screenshot as background with a semi-transparent dark overlay and bold white label text at the top. 
Large readable typography. 
Smooth animated transitions. 
No audio needed (designed for silent viewing with captions).
```
