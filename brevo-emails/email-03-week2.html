Two weeks in. By now you should have a feel for the basics. This email is about the things people discover a little later and then wonder how they managed without.

01  /  Finance Module
Most church admins spend hours reconciling tithes and contributions manually each month. In Kereke Hub, every contribution is logged against a member and a category. At month-end, the export takes 30 seconds. If your treasurer handles finances separately, invite them in and assign the Finance role.

02  /  WhatsApp Broadcast
Under Communications, you can draft a message and send it as a WhatsApp broadcast to your congregation. This works directly from the dashboard - no separate app needed.

03  /  Visitor Pipeline
If you track first-time visitors, the Visitors module gives them a formal follow-up pipeline. You can log when they were contacted, whether they came back, and what their current status is. A simple way to make sure nobody falls through the cracks.
