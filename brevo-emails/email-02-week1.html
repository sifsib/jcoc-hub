Most church admins tell us the two things that make the biggest difference in the first week are:

• Getting the member roster started. Even a partial list helps. Once your members are in, attendance, communications, and analytics all start working.

A few things worth knowing

You can upload members from Excel. Go to Roster and look for the Import button. You don't have to enter people manually.

Taking attendance takes about two minutes per service once your roster is in.

You can invite other team members to help. You don't have to do this alone.
