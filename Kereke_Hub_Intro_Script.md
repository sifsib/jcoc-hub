## FEATURE OVERVIEW

### Member Roster
Your full congregation directory in one place. Capture detailed member information, create family groups, manage roles and ministry assignments, and keep everything organized and accessible.

### Attendance Analytics
Log attendance per service or cell group and let the system do the analysis. Track attendance trends automatically, spot members who might be drifting away, and get real-time insights into the health of your congregation.

### Financial Tracking
Log tithes, Sunday collections, building funds, and special offerings. Export anytime in CSV or Excel for your bank auditor or bank reconciliation. Everything auditable, everything in order.

### Visitor Pipeline
First-time guests are logged with contact details and tracked through a pipeline stage. Know who visited, when they visited, and where they are in the follow-up process.

### Events Management
Create and manage church events. Track RSVPs, set capacity limits per event, and keep everyone informed about what's happening next in your church calendar.

### Groups and Cell Management
Organise members into cell groups, departments, or ministry teams. Each group has its own member list, leader assignment, and independent attendance tracking. Perfect for large churches or decentralized ministries.

### Automated Workflows
Build multi-step follow-up sequences that run automatically based on triggers. A new visitor is logged and the workflow follows. A member gives their life to Christ and a welcome process starts. No step missed. No one forgotten.

### Custom Forms
Build shareable forms for new member registrations, event signups, prayer requests, or whatever your church needs. Put them on your website or share via link. Data comes straight into your system.

### Communications
Send bulk emails directly from your church Gmail address using your own SMTP credentials. Keep everyone informed without leaving the system.

### Team and Role Management
Invite church staff and leaders as admin users. Control what each person can see and do inside your workspace. Your office manager sees finances and members. Your youth leader sees their group. Everyone has the right access.

### Volunteer Scheduling
Roster volunteers to roles for each service or event. Know who's covering sound, who's greeting, who's leading worship next Sunday. Coordination without chaos.

### Worship Planning
Build and manage your church song library with keys, tempo notes, and chord chart links. Plan your setlist weeks in advance and share it with your musicians.

### Online Giving
Accept tithes and offerings online via PayFast card payments or manual EFT. Make giving easy for your members and get direct deposits into your church account.

### Support Ticketing
Raise a support request directly from your dashboard and get a response from the Kereke Hub team via email. No waiting on chat queues. No searching for a contact number. Real support when you need it.
