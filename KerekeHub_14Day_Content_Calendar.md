## Notes for Copywriter

- All hooks above are **working titles** — creative copy is the next phase
- Day 2 and Day 9 videos can be screen recordings with voiceover (no on-camera required)
- Day 14 LinkedIn video should end with an explicit CTA: Book at kerekehub.co.za/demo
- Engagement posts (Days 3, 6, 10, 13) must include a clear reply prompt or tag mechanic to drive comments
- Brand colours: dark green `#1A5C38`, mid green `#2E7D4F`, slate text `#2C3E50`
